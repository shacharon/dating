## 3. COVERAGE & GAPS ANALYSIS

### ✅ Well-Covered Psychological Areas:
1. **Attachment & Emotional Security** — attachmentSecurity, emotionalDepth, fusion/boundaries
2. **Communication Style** — directness, conflictStyle
3. **Values Alignment** — traditionalism, spirituality, relationshipClarity
4. **Lifestyle Compatibility** — lifestylePace, socialBattery, independence
5. **Practical Alignment** — financialMindset, ambition

### ⚠️ MISSING or UNDER-REPRESENTED Areas:

#### A. Core Emotional Intelligence Signals (MISSING)
- **Empathy / Compassion** — ability to understand others' feelings
- **Emotional Regulation** — managing own emotions, not reactive
- **Self-Awareness** — understanding own patterns, growth mindset
- **Emotional Expression** — comfort showing affection, love languages
- **Playfulness / Humor Style** — fun, lightness, banter compatibility

#### B. Relationship Skills (MISSING)
- **Listening Quality** — active listening, presence
- **Repair Skills** — ability to apologize, make amends after fights
- **Vulnerability Capacity** — willingness to be open/authentic
- **Growth Mindset** — openness to change, learning in relationship
- **Patience / Tolerance** — how they handle partner's flaws

#### C. Life Stage & Goals (UNDER-COVERED)
- **Parenthood Readiness** — want kids, timeline urgency
- **Career Priority** — work-life balance, ambition impact on relationship
- **Geographic Flexibility** — willing to relocate vs rooted
- **Adventure vs Stability** — novelty-seeking vs routine comfort
- **Intellectual Curiosity** — learning, ideas, intellectual stimulation needs

#### D. Sexual & Intimacy (MISSING)
- **Physical Affection Style** — touch frequency, PDA comfort
- **Sexual Compatibility** — libido match, openness/experimentation
- **Intimacy Pace** — slow vs fast to physical/emotional intimacy
- **Monogamy Alignment** — relationship structure expectations

#### E. Conflict & Stress (UNDER-COVERED)
- **Conflict Avoidance** — withdraws vs engages in conflict
- **Stress Response** — how they act under pressure/stress
- **Forgiveness Style** — holds grudges vs quick to forgive
- **Jealousy / Security** — trust, possessiveness, jealousy patterns

#### F. Daily Life Compatibility (PARTIAL)
- **Cleanliness / Organization** — neat freak vs messy
- **Morning/Night Person** — sleep schedule compatibility
- **Alone Time Needs** — recharge solo vs together
- **Planning vs Spontaneity** — structured vs go-with-flow
- **Food / Dietary** — eating habits, food importance

#### G. Family & Social (MISSING)
- **Family Closeness** — family-oriented vs distant from family
- **Friend Priority** — solo time with friends vs couple-centric
- **Social Events** — party/events vs quiet nights preference
- **Community Involvement** — volunteering, causes, activism

## 5. TECHNICAL CONSIDERATIONS

### A. Signal Extraction Challenges
- **Text-based signals** (current approach) work for: ambition, values, emotional depth
- **Harder to extract from text**: empathy, listening skills, stress response
- **May need**: Direct survey questions for some new signals
- **Hybrid approach**: Text analysis + targeted questions for gaps

### B. Weighting Strategy
- Current highest weights: `emotionalDepth`, `financialMindset`, `spirituality`, `lifestylePace` (1.5)
- New high-priority signals should be weighted 1.3-1.5:
  - `empathyCompassion` → 1.5 (foundational)
  - `vulnerabilityOpenness` → 1.4 (critical for depth)
  - `repairSkills` → 1.3 (relationship longevity)
  - `parenthoodAlignment` → 1.5 (dealbreaker territory)

### C. Tension Rules Expansion
New tensions to consider:
- `empathy_gap` — One highly empathetic, other struggles (gap ≥5) — penalty 4
- `stress_mismatch` — One seeks support under stress, other withdraws — penalty 5
- `cleanliness_clash` — Extreme neatness vs extreme messiness — penalty 3
- `kid_timeline_mismatch` — Want kids but very different timelines — penalty 6
- `intellectual_gap` — Big mismatch in intellectual curiosity/stimulation needs — penalty 3
